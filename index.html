THE ASSEMBLY

Every part, defined.

Inspect the build. Take it to the workshop.

A clean shell. An independent frame.

The 2 mm 316L chamber has no leg mounts, screw housings or bracket welds. A separate cage supports its perimeter on removable silicone strips.

  1. Fabricate the collector. Cut four walls and four developed floor panels from the DXF. Fixture to 1200 × 800; flush TIG weld, blend R3 internal seams, finish Ra ≤ 0.8 µm, pickle and passivate.
  2. Fit the drain. Weld the Ø53.8 × 1.5 tube on centre, flush with the floor. The 130 mm straight drop has no elbows or internal ledges.
  3. Build the cage. Weld the 30 × 30 × 2 tube cut list. Fit four cage-only adjustable legs, silicone strips and removable lid restraints. Never drill the chamber.
  4. Install the product stand. Two solid perpendicular support sheets form a removable L. Saddle feet maintain a drainage gap; the front and right sides stay open.
  5. Seal the canopy. Place the continuous U-gasket on the inclined rim. Fit the 6 mm glass and cage pressure bars. The canopy extends 50 mm beyond every chamber edge.
  6. Connect collection. Fit the wide-mouth vessel, straight sleeve, cap face seal and dry filtered vent. Adjust the shelf with the cage so all drain datums remain aligned.

Open. Brush. Rinse. Inspect.

Clean after every batch. Let the assembly cool, then move it to a stable cleaning surface.

  1. Remove the vessel; cap its ports. Release the cage pressure bars and lift the glass with two people. Lift off the rim gasket.
  2. Lift out the tray, L-stand and all four silicone feet. Both stand sides are directly accessible. Remove the drain sleeve, cap and vent cartridge.
  3. Wash with a food-equipment detergent at its labelled dilution and temperature. Brush the Ø50.8 bore straight through with a 45–50 mm nylon brush.
  4. Rinse with potable water, then use a compatible food-contact sanitizer following its contact time and rinse instructions. Avoid chloride cleaners and thermal shock to glass.
  5. Inspect under light: no residue in seams, free drainage, intact silicone, dry undamaged vent. Air dry all parts, then reassemble the closed path.

A passive, closed vapour path

Sunlight heats the sliced product. Vapour condenses on the cooler canopy and chamber surfaces; gravity carries condensate to the four-plane collector and straight drop. The sealed bottle sits behind a three-sided sun shield. A hydrophobic vent balances pressure.

The enclosure reduces exposure to insects, rain and dust; it cannot guarantee sterility. Prototype condensate is not certified drinking water. Confirm drying time, product water activity and water quality through appropriate testing; do not assume sunlight alone sanitizes food.
