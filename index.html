🛠️ Core Architectural Blueprint

  • Sanitary Architecture: The main 316 Stainless Steel chamber has absolutely zero internal or external welded leg-mount brackets, threads, or crevices. This completely eliminates hidden food-residue traps, making cleaning entirely seamless.
  • Inner Product Stand: The inner structural stand is formed by exactly 2 solid perpendicular support planes. The remaining two sides are completely open, allowing water to cascade unhindered down the short, large-bore vertical drop drain directly into the glass bottle below. This smooth, open design avoids sharp inside corners or stagnant fluid pools.
  • External Exoskeleton Legs: Detachable support legs form a self-locking structural cage beneath the main chamber. They wrap tightly around the exterior shell and lock together securely without piercing or welding onto the main chamber walls. They support any required vessel height to accommodate various bottle sizes in the cool shade underneath.
  • Rain & Dust Overlap Lid: The heavy top glass plate is oversized to wrap securely around the steel chassis flange. It acts as an architectural awning that effortlessly deflects rain and atmospheric dust away from the system seals, ensuring zero cross-contamination.

📐 Material Specifications

  • Chassis & Piping: Food-Grade 316 Stainless Steel (Excellent tolerance to high-temperature moisture and localized chloride salt pitting).
  • Condenser Lid: Tempered Low-Iron Solar Glass (High optical clarity, excellent thermal retention, and scratch-proof durability).
  • Collection Flask: High-Borosilicate Glass Bottle (Zero microplastic leaching, immune to aggressive mineral-free distilled water).
  • Exoskeleton Assembly: Structural Marine-Grade Aluminum or Coated Steel.

🧼 Operational Cleaning Protocol

  • Disconnect the 5-liter collection bottle at the sealed cap interface.
  • Pour boiling water directly into the large-diameter perpendicular sink hole.
  • Pass a straight-line structural nylon brush cleanly from top to bottom.
  • Rinse the open 2-plane internal product stand without disassembling any elements.
